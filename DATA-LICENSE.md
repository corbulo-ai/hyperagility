# Data license and credit

HyperAgility is free to use and build on. Please credit both the original paper authors and this site.

## Credit the original authors

The findings, titles and quotes in this data come from the papers' authors. If you use a specific result or quote, cite the original paper and respect its license. Each paper's link is in `public/papers.csv`.

## Credit HyperAgility

The structured data in `public/kg.json.gz` and `public/papers.csv` (the selection and arrangement of results, the coding and categories, and the derived statistics) is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). If you reuse it, credit this site with a link. You can use this wording:

> Source: HyperAgility (hyperagility.org), based on results from the cited papers.

## What this license does not cover

Paper titles, abstracts and quoted passages remain the work of their original authors and publishers. They are included in short form, with attribution, so that readers can identify each source and check each result. This license grants no rights in that material, so reuse of it is subject to the original paper's terms.

Paper PDFs and full text are not distributed here.

## Corrections and removals

If a result is wrong, or you are an author and want something changed or removed, please [open an issue](https://github.com/corbulo-ai/hyperagility/issues).
